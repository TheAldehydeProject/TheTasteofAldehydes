### Nicholas Nilsson
- text: Nicholas Nilsson is an American perfumer, born in 1995, who grew up among the forests of the Rocky Mountains. He founded Pineward in 2020, at twenty-five, meaning it to be a line of perfumes of pine and fir.

### Dan & Roxana Adar
- text: Dan and Roxana Adar are the two in-house perfumers of ADAR, a European house with Romanian roots. The house says it came out of a long dialogue between two creators who work as The Light and The Dark — one drawn to the luminous and ethereal, the other to depth, shadow and intensity.
- text: For many years its perfumes went only to a small circle of private collectors, as commissions; it has been public since 2025. Every core formula is developed in-house by the two of them, each bottle is made by hand from marble, stone, wood, resin or metal, and each perfume is made in numbered micro-batches with one note left undisclosed. The house also works with a few outside perfumers, Antoine Lie, Miguel Matos and Manuel Attardi among them.

### Miguel Matos
- text: Miguel Matos is a Portuguese perfumer who works from a small laboratory and showroom of his own in Almada, near Lisbon. For about twenty years he was a journalist of the arts — an editor at Time Out Portugal, a co-founder of the culture magazine Umbigo, a writer for Vogue Portugal — and he is a scent critic and a curator of scent art as well.
- text: He taught himself perfumery from books until the perfumer Christian Carbonnel took him into his laboratory and became his mentor. His own label, Miguel Matos Perfumes, began in 2018. He has made perfumes for Nishane, Sarah Baker (Jungle Jezebel), Astrophil & Stella (Sweet Pulp), CALAJ and Ricardo Ramos, and he is creative director and in-house perfumer of Bruno Acampora. The Fragrance Foundation, the Perfumed Plume Awards and the Art and Olfaction Awards have all recognised his work.

### Goran Bajazetov
- text: Goran Bajazetov is the founder, perfumer and creative director of Almost Human. Before perfume he spent more than twenty years in design, visual design, storytelling and the making of technology products.
- text: He founded the house in 2025. Its perfumes draw on science fiction, on esoteric ideas and on work across disciplines, and they are told not by a pyramid of notes but by how they change on the skin. The first collection, First Signal, came out in 2026: Burning Bridges, Dear Future, Desert Hope, Ritual Code and Silent Rain. Parfumo and Pitti Immagine give the house as Romanian; Scent Verdict places it in Novi Sad, in Serbia.

### David-Lev Jipa-Slivinschi
- text: David-Lev Jipa-Slivinschi was born in Bucharest, of Russian and Ukrainian heritage. He studied film and screenwriting, and came to perfume during the pandemic.

### Antoine Lie
- text: Antoine Lie is a French perfumer, raised in Strasbourg, who studied chemistry before moving to Grasse to study perfume, and was apprenticed to the teacher Jean Guichard. He worked in France and in the United States, where he took part in Armani Code, Versace Crystal Noir and Ralph Lauren Romance for Men, at Givaudan, at IFF and, from 2011, at Takasago.
- text: Wanting more freedom, he left the large companies for niche houses, and since 2018 has been independent, with a studio of his own in Paris, ALOE — Antoine Lie Olfactive Experience. He calls himself an abstract perfumer. He has made perfumes for ERIS, Houbigant, Les Indémodables and Zoologist, Trudon's Absolu, Comme des Garçons' Wonderwood and État Libre d'Orange's Rien — and every perfume of Les Abstraits, with its founder, Eugen Nizic.

### Mathew Schmuelian
- text: Mathew Schmuelian is the perfumer and founder of Lost Tribe, a house of natural perfume he started in New York City in 2022. Before it he spent several years collecting artisanal natural perfumes, which led him to source his own raw materials and to macerate animalic ones himself.
- text: Lost Tribe is all natural and handmade in New York. Its perfumes are dense, with prominent animalic notes — musk, oud and ambergris — and an unapologetically intense style; he has described its aim as drawing attention to a niche corner of perfumery and teaching people about all-natural perfume. Pan's Labyrinth he made with the perfumer Rajesh Balkrishnan.

### Rajesh Balkrishnan
- text: Rajesh Balkrishnan is an independent perfumer who is also an academic, a professor of public health at the University of Virginia School of Medicine, in Charlottesville. He came to perfume from medical research, and learned it mostly by himself and by working with others.
- text: His perfumes are dense and woody, often animalic, and often drawn from Indian culture and nature. Among them are Sayat Nova, made with Dmitry Bortnikoff after the eighteenth-century Armenian poet, and Aranyaka, made with Prin Lomros. Parfumo knows twenty-two perfumes by him; for Lost Tribe he made Pan's Labyrinth, with Mathew Schmuelian.

### Holladay Penick Saltz
- text: Holladay Penick Saltz is from the area of Jackson, Mississippi. She graduated from the Rhode Island School of Design and studied fashion and material culture at Parsons and The New School, then studied the formulation and making of perfume at Le Labo in New York.

### Christopher Sheldrake
- text: Christopher Sheldrake is a British perfumer, born on 11 April 1955 in Madras, India. Training as an architect, he went to France to improve his French and found himself in Grasse, and in perfume; the perfumer Georges Cœur took him on as an apprentice in 1974, and he worked at Chanel and for many years at Quest International.
- text: In 1989 he met Serge Lutens, then Shiseido's artistic director. The Salons du Palais-Royal line opened in 1992 with his Féminité du Bois, and nearly the whole of the Serge Lutens line is his, Ambre Sultan and Chergui among it. In 2005 he returned to Chanel as perfumer and director of research and development, where Coco Noir is his; he has made perfumes for Rochas, Avon and Space NK as well.

### François Demachy
- text: François Demachy is a French perfumer, born in Cannes and raised in Grasse, where his father ran a pharmacy. He studied dentistry and physiotherapy, worked at Mane, and then trained for five years at the perfumery school of Charabot.
- text: At Chanel he became director of research and development, working at the same time on the perfumes of Chanel, Ungaro, Bourjois and Tiffany. In 2006 Dior made him its perfumer-creator and director of development for the perfumes and cosmetics of LVMH, where he also worked for Fendi and Acqua di Parma, and remade Miss Dior and the Eau Sauvage and Dior Homme lines. Francis Kurkdjian succeeded him at Dior in 2021, and he stayed on as a perfumer within LVMH.

### Ramón Monegal
- text: Ramón Monegal, born in Barcelona in 1951, is the fourth generation of a family of perfumers: his great-grandfather, Esteve Monegal i Prat, opened the drugstore that became Myrurgia in 1916, the house that supplied the Spanish royal family.
- text: He studied architecture first, and began training as a perfumer in 1972 at the family company, and then in Geneva with Artur Jordi Pey, in Grasse with Marcel Carles and in Paris with Pierre Bourdon. For more than thirty years he made perfumes at Myrurgia, many of them best-sellers in Spain, and colognes for Adolfo Domínguez, Antonio Miró and Massimo Dutti. After Puig took Myrurgia over he stepped away, and in 2009 he founded his own house, Monegal, in Barcelona; his book La Perfumista came out in 2012.

### Sultan Pasha
- text: Sultan Pasha is an independent perfumer and maker of attars in London, who grew up between Dhaka and London and has a background in chemistry. Before making perfume he collected and sold rare vintage perfumes.

His style draws on French perfumery before the 1960s, with heavy use of rare natural materials. ### Christian Carbonnel
- text: Christian Carbonnel, called Chris Maurice by some of the houses he works for — both are his real names, his full name being Christian Maurice Carbonnel — is the third generation of a Spanish family of perfumers. His grandfather, the French perfumer Maurice Carbonnel, founded Carbonnel S.A. in Spain in 1925.

### Josh Lobb
- text: Josh Lobb is an entirely self-taught perfumer in Portland, Oregon. Slumberhouse began there as a collective among artist friends, he making the perfumes and they the art, clothing and the rest; his perfumes, sold one at a time on Etsy, took off, and it became his own house.
