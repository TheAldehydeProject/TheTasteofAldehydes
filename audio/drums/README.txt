THE DRUMS — the kit the drum staves on Qimu & Musicians play (qimu.js).

One stroke of each: kick (bass drum), snare, side (a cross-stick on the snare), tom1 (high tom),
tom2 (low tom; the floor tom is this one played lower), hh (hi-hat struck closed), hho (hi-hat
open), hhp (hi-hat closed by the foot), crash, ride, and bell (the ride's bell). From the Versilian
Community Sample Library (github.com/sgossner/VCSL): Bass Drum 1, Snare Drum Modern 1 and 2, Tom 1
and 2 (stick), Hi-Hat Cymbal, Suspended Cymbal 1 and 2 — each trimmed from where it is struck,
levelled to the same loudness, faded out and made mono.
