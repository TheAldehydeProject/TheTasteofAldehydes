THE PIANO — the notes the staves on Qimu & Musicians play (qimu.js).

Seventeen notes of the Salamander Grand Piano, a recording of a Yamaha C5
grand piano by Alexander Holm, a minor third apart from C2 to C6 ("Ds" is
D sharp, "Fs" F sharp). A note between two of them is the nearer one
played a little higher or lower. These copies are the ones Tone.js
publishes (github.com/Tonejs/audio, salamander/).
