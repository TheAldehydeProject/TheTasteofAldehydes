THE GUITAR — the notes the guitar staves on Qimu & Musicians play (qimu.js).

Fourteen notes of a nylon-string classical guitar, from D2 to A-sharp 5 ("Cs" is C sharp, "Ds" D
sharp, "Fs" F sharp, "As" A sharp), multisampled by quartertone and published on Freesound
(freesound.org, pack 11573, "classicalguitar-multisampled"). These copies are the ones the
tonejs-instruments project publishes (github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments, samples/guitar-nylon),
trimmed to their first three seconds and faded out. A note between two of them is the nearer one
played a little higher or lower.
