Mapping the notes of the houses · test page 2

    Every house on the site is a dome down the arc, and every note its fragrances name is a tick round it, in its accord — the sixteen families of the Note Library, laid round as a year is laid round its months.

    From each house a line runs out to every accord its notes are in, as bright as that accord's share of them. Point at a house, or at an accord, and the rest steps back.

    The Sixteen Accords

      How to Use It

      Scroll, pinch or double-click to come closer, and drag to move about; the + and − at the foot do the same, and Fit shows the whole again. The closer you come, the more of the notes are named, with how many fragrances name each.

      Press a house, an accord — on the arc or in the list below — or, closer in, a single note, and what it is opens here: a house's accords, notes and fragrances; an accord's houses and notes; a note's houses, the fragrances that name it and what it is most often with. Every name in it opens its own.

      How to Read It

      A dome turns as its house's fragrances go round it; the path over it rises with how many there are. A tick is a note, longer the more fragrances name it. The bands deepen from the domes outward, as the arc of the poster this is after does from night to day.
