A Portrait of the Perfumers

Every perfume on The Taste of Aldehydes and in Prin Lomros' houses, by the perfumer who made it and the house it was made for — a thread for each, rising from the perfumer to the house, from the site's Perfumers ledger.

How it is made

Who made each perfume, and when, is the site's Perfumers ledger, a source for every one; where none names a perfumer they stand together as Perfumer not found. Point at a perfumer, a house or a thread; press one for its panel.
