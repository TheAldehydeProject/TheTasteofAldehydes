      Intro

      Every perfume written about on this site, traced back to the person who made it — and the whole catalogue of Prin Lomros, whose Haxan is one of them.

      The Earth stands in the middle, and three rings stand out from it, a place in each for every perfume: its perfumer, its house, and the perfume itself — a bar out to its year, the further out, the more recent. Choose a perfumer — on the circle or in the list — and their houses appear; scroll, and go through their perfumes.

      Legendwhat the marks mean
      • The Earth — in the middle, turning to what is chosen
      • A house's country — the country the ledger gives, never a town
      • A perfumer's country
      • A line from a house's country to each of its perfumes on the rings
      • Ring 1, the perfumer — named by a source
      • The house's perfumer — no nose named for this one
      • Not publicly available — no perfumer named
      • Ring 2, the house — an individual fragrance written about here
      • a house perfume written about here
      • Prin Lomros' houses, not written about here
      • Ring 3, the perfume — a bar as long as its year, 2009 shortest to 2026
      • Year not found — on the dashed ring

      Termsthe words the chart uses
      Perfumer
      The person who composes a perfume; also called its nose.
      House
      The name a perfume is made and sold under.
      Named
      A source names this perfumer as the maker of this perfume.
      The house's perfumer
      A source names this person as the house's own, or only, perfumer — but none names the nose of this one perfume.
      Not publicly available
      Who made it is not publicly available. The chart leaves it empty rather than guess.
      Not found
      No source found gives the year. The chart leaves it on the dashed ring rather than guess.
      Year
      The year a source gives for a perfume's first release; a later version is noted beside it.
      *Disclaimerwhere the chart's facts come from

      Who made a perfume, and when, is taken only from the sources at the foot of this page. Where none could be found the chart says so rather than guess — a hollow mark has no year, and the perfumes no source credits stand together under Not publicly available.
