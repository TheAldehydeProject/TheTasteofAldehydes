First Iteration: A Flacon for Every Perfumer

  • every perfumer behind a perfume on The Taste of Aldehydes, and Prin Lomros' three houses — a flacon each, designed from what they made.
  • Height — a millilitre a perfume: 139 ml the tallest.
  • Body — its perfumes by year, the earliest at the foot, the newest at the shoulder; it swells where most were made.
  • Graduations up the wall, one a perfume — long: named by a source; short: the house's perfumer; dotted: not found.
  • Juice, the right half cut away — a layer for each house, as deep as what was made for it.
  • Cap — a tier for each house. Label — how sure the sources are.
  • Family — where its perfumes are: round, the individual fragrances; square, a house's on the site; faceted, Prin Lomros' houses.
  • Under the shelf, each one's plan, from above.
  • Press a flacon; press it again for a layer or a graduation.
