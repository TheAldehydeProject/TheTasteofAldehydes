Lost Tribe
A House of Notable Quality

00

Introduction

This is yet another one of those houses that chooses the quality of the ingredients as its unique selling point, and I do think that they deliver. Despite that though, I find that the prices are quite absurd even for what the fragrances are.

The ideas are creative, and the execution is really good, which is precisely why I choose to write about Lost Tribe. I also find that because the ingredients are quite high end, the complexity of these fragrances naturally rivals other VERY complex fragrance houses. For this reason, I feel the need to emphasize that I had but a brief chance of smelling all of these fragrances, and so the descriptions given are general placeholders until I smell the fragrances wholly, and give them enough time to properly be evaluated.

01 Kong Open

Starts super abrasive, but turns green and banana-y, in a really REALLY well blended manner. It does have some sharp woody animalics too. Honestly, it is a good gorilla fragrance. I do see it. There is quite a bit of ambrette too. There is spices and a LOT of oud, but not in an overwhelming manner.

02 Watch King Open

Smells really really smooth at the top, despite being aromatic and complex. Leather, Peru balsam and something else. The aromatic facet developed into a leather and oudy fragrance eventually. Also balsamic. I cannot think of anything that this smells like. It is quite unique.

03 Pan’s Labyrinth Open

Abrasive animalics, woods and ambers. Good.

04 Dirty Soda Open

This is a really impressive creation. It smells like one of those artisanal sodas that smell slightly herbal despite still being black drinks full of sugar and carbonic acid. I found this one to be particularly similar to the taste of Fentiman’s Curiosity Cola.

05 Equatorial Open

Incense, pepper lavender, mint and a bunch of fir. This is like a very complex Pineward on aromatic and spicy overdrive. It is really good honestly. Also the fir is an accord composite of a gajillion other things; but I dont know which exactly. Evergreen oleoresins probably.

06 Santal Absolu Open

Resin, vanilla, A LOT OF AROMATICS, incense, ylang ylang and of course sandalwood! I dont know what else. it smells quite tame relative to what I expected
