    Intro

    Every perfume written about on this site, traced back to the person who made it — and the whole catalogue of Prin Lomros, whose Haxan is one of them.

    The perfumers stand round the circle, and every perfume is a line out from the middle: the further out, the more recent. Choose a perfumer — on the circle or in the list — and their houses appear; choose a house, and its perfumes are written out.

    Disclaimer

    Who made a perfume, and when, is taken only from the sources at the foot of this page. Where none could be found the chart says so rather than guess — a hollow mark has no year, and the perfumes no source credits stand together under Perfumer not found.

    Terms

    Perfumer
    The person who composes a perfume; also called its nose.
    House
    The name a perfume is made and sold under.
    Named
    A source names this perfumer as the maker of this perfume.
    The house's perfumer
    A source names this person as the house's own, or only, perfumer — but none names the nose of this one perfume.
    Not found
    No source found says. The chart leaves it empty rather than guess.
    Year
    The year a source gives for a perfume's first release; a later version is noted beside it.
